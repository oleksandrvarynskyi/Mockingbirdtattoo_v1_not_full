Hi there!
I’m Viktoria.

I’m a tattoo artist working in a graphic and fine line style. I absolutely love creating botanical and floral tattoos. Delicate pieces, fine lines, and soft shading are what I enjoy the most.

My second direction is X-ray flowers. This style makes people fall in love with it from the very first glance. I truly love creating these incredible translucent flowers with soft contrast and detail.

A little about me:
I’m originally from Ukraine andhave been living in Calgary for the past few years. I truly love this city, the mountains, and the amazing people here.

Message me and I’ll help bring your idea to life and create a custom design just for you.
